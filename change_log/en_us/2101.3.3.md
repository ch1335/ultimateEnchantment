# 2101.3.3 Update

## Nerfed Enchantments

- Cut Down: Reduced the damage bonus gained per 1% of health difference [(0.02% x enchantment level) -> (0.01% x enchantment level)]. The bonus cap is unchanged
- Cut Down: Now grants a damage modifier based on the current health difference between you and the target, instead of a final damage multiplier

## Adjusted Enchantments

- Ultimate Slayer: Now grants a damage modifier and bonus loot instead
