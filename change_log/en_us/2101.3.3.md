# 2101.3.3 Update

## New Enchantments

- Trail: Helmet enchantment. Grants 10% Seeking per level, up to level 3
- Spread: When mining ore, spreads across connected ore blocks. 2 extra blocks per level, up to level 3
- Lucky Mind: Helmet enchantment. Grants +0.25 Fortune, +0.25 Looting and +0.5 Luck per level, up to level 4

## Nerfed Enchantments

- Cut Down: Reduced the damage bonus gained per 1% of health difference [(0.02% x enchantment level) -> (0.01% x enchantment level)]. The bonus cap is unchanged
- Cut Down: Now grants a damage modifier based on the current health difference between you and the target, instead of a final damage multiplier

## Adjusted Enchantments

- Ultimate Slayer: Now grants a damage modifier and bonus loot instead

## Other Changes

- Optimized how some enchantments apply damage

## Dependencies

- ArcanumLib (`arcanumlib`) 1.21.1-0.2.0 or newer is now a required dependency. A missing dependency error is reported at load time if it is absent
