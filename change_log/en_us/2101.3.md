# 2101.3 Major Update

## Core Rework

- The enchantment backend has been almost entirely reworked, now using a brand-new enchantment registration system.

## New Enchantments

- Ultimate Slayer: Grants damage and loot bonuses against bosses. Damage is (3 x lvl)% and loot is (5 x lvl)%, calculated separately for each piece of equipment by its own level. Apotheosis bosses also count as bosses
- The Fortress: While held, copies (10 x lvl)% of your armor's attributes
- Hurt Deepens: When an arrow hits, deals 1% extra damage for every 1% the target's current health percentage is below yours, up to (10 x lvl)%
- Ethereal Arrow: Arrows deal (0.1 x lvl) extra damage per block flown, up to (4 x lvl)

## Buffed Enchantments

- Tear: Increased the damage calculated from the target's current health percentage (0.1%->1%)
- Vanquisher: Removed the "effective attack" concept in favor of a fixed cooldown, and now grants 4% melee lifesteal at max stacks
- Last Stand: Increased the trigger threshold (33.3%->40%)
- Thunderbolt: Increased the damage the third attack deals to other enemies in range ((10 x lvl)% -> (25 x lvl)%)

## Nerfed Enchantments

- Scabbing: Reworked the formula. Armor shred no longer scales linearly with level ((5 x lvl)% -> (1 - Math.pow(1-0.04,lvl))%)

## Adjusted Enchantments

- Life Steal: Reduced the level scaling of the lifesteal ratio ((2.5 x lvl)%->(3 + lvl)%), and increased the per-hit heal cap (scales with level, reaching its maximum of 8% at level 4)
- Mana Steal: Reduced the level scaling of the mana steal ratio ((10 x lvl)%->(4 + 2 x lvl)%), and increased the per-hit restore cap (scales with level, reaching its maximum of 10% at level 5)

## Other Changes

- All ultimate enchantments can no longer have their levels raised by conventional means, such as Endersurge from Apotheosis.
- Added a very convenient way to configure enchantments, which also allows using lines of JS code to define the formulas.
- Slightly lowered the enchanting level requirements of some enchantments.
