# 2101.1.3.1 Update

## New Enchantments

- Pierce Through: Armor shred for bows and crossbows, (1 - Math.pow(1-0.05,lvl))%, about 22.6% at max level
- Harvest: Grants (15 x lvl)% extra drops when breaking mature crops with a hoe

## Buffed Enchantments

- Ethereal Arrow, Hurt Deepens: Now apply to crossbows as well as bows

## Adjusted Enchantments

- Scabbing, Pierce Through: Fixed incorrect attribute modifiers. Armor shred now stacks as a flat value instead of a percentage of the base value
